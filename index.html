Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our booking procedures and event coordination service.

You can book an event by filling out the Online Booking Form below with details like date, guests, budget, and event type. Our lead planner will review it and contact you within 24 hours to schedule an initial consultation call.

We recommend booking at least 3 to 6 months in advance for large celebrations like weddings or corporate summits. For smaller birthdays or private parties, 1 month is usually sufficient, depending on venue availability.

Yes, absolutely! Our packages serve as initial budget guidelines. You can add or remove services (e.g. adding drone photography, selecting specific decorations, or choosing customized menus) as needed. We will prepare a custom proposal for you.

We accept all major credit cards, bank wire transfers, and secure online checkouts. Usually, we require a 30% initial deposit to reserve the date and venue, with installments scheduled leading up to the event day.

Yes, we provide fully-integrated decoration, gourmet catering, professional sounds, live DJ music, and multi-angle camera coverage. You can choose to use our in-house teams or hire external vendors coordinates through our managers.

Bookings can be rescheduled up to 30 days prior to the event date without any extra penalty, depending on venue slots. Cancellations made more than 45 days in advance are eligible for a partial deposit refund.
